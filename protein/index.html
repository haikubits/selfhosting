Post-Surgery Recovery

Weekly Protein Diet Plan

Strictly vegetarian · No dairy curd or dahi · ~50g protein daily · Designed for gentle digestion after gallbladder removal

✅ Dietary Rules
  • No eggs, fish, or meat
  • No curd, dahi, yogurt, or hung curd (allergy)
  • No high-fat foods (post-surgery)
  • Low oil — boiled or lightly sautéed only
  • Small, frequent portions preferred
⚠️ Post-Surgery Notes
  • Rajma and chhole only after 3–4 weeks
  • Always pressure-cook legumes thoroughly
  • Squeeze excess water from soya chunks
  • If loose stools occur, reduce legumes temporarily
  • Hydrate well between meals

Prep Instructions
  1. Soak soya chunks in warm water for 20 min. Boil in fresh water for 10 min. Squeeze out all excess water to reduce bloating risk.
  2. Lightly sauté soya with cumin, salt, and minimal oil (1–2 sprays). Avoid heavy spices this week.
  3. Use silken tofu if available — crumble it into the meal or eat as is with a pinch of salt and lemon.
  4. Drink milk warm, ideally between meals rather than with a heavy meal.

Prep Instructions
  1. Use split yellow moong dal (not whole green moong) — it is significantly easier to digest post-surgery.
  2. Pressure cook until very soft (3–4 whistles). Season with only turmeric, salt, and a small cumin tadka in minimal ghee (½ tsp max).
  3. Eat cottage cheese at room temperature — cold food can cause discomfort post-surgery. Mix with cucumber or a pinch of chaat masala for taste.
  4. Warm milk can be taken before bed as the day's final protein top-up.

Prep Instructions
  1. Sprout moong at home: soak whole green moong overnight, drain, tie in a damp cloth, leave for 24–36 hrs until tails appear.
  2. Lightly steam or blanch the sprouts — do not eat raw in the first 4 weeks post-surgery as raw sprouts can cause gas and bloating.
  3. Pan-fry tofu in a non-stick pan with minimal oil, salt, and turmeric. No heavy masalas.
  4. Mix all three into a light bowl with lemon juice and coriander for a simple protein salad.

Prep Instructions
  1. Soak rajma overnight (minimum 8 hrs). This reduces lectins and makes them significantly easier to digest.
  2. Pressure cook for at least 5–6 whistles until very soft. Mash a few beans in the pot to make it a thicker, easier-to-digest consistency.
  3. Use minimal oil for the tadka — just cumin, ginger, and tomato. Skip heavy cream or butter completely.
  4. Bhuna chana can be eaten as a between-meal snack if protein needs to be topped up.

Prep Instructions

General Reminders
💧
Drink 2–2.5L of water daily. Without a gallbladder, bile flows continuously, and hydration helps fat digestion.
🍽️
Eat 4–5 small meals rather than 2–3 large ones. Large meals are harder without the bile reserve from the gallbladder.
🚫
Avoid fried foods, heavy ghee, cream, or coconut oil for at least 4–6 weeks. Fat digestion is the primary challenge post-surgery.
🌿
Ginger and ajwain (carom seeds) in cooking help significantly with post-surgery gas and bloating.
📊
Calories listed are for protein sources only. Add roti, rice, or fruit to meet your total daily calorie needs.
👨‍⚕️
Consult your surgeon or dietitian before introducing rajma and chhole. Recovery timelines vary by individual.
